Questions

How Skiff compares venues, what you pay, and what can go wrong.

What is Skiff?+

A swap for stock tokens on Robinhood Chain. Type an amount, and before you sign you see what every venue gives you, side by side, each one simulated on the same block. You can pay from Robinhood Chain or from another chain, and you sign from your own wallet.

+

+

What does Skiff charge?+

Our fee is 0%. We add nothing to any route. You still pay others, and every line is shown before you sign: the route fee (a percent of the amount, already taken out of what you get, 0.25% on recent routes), a bridge fee when you pay from another chain, and network gas from your wallet. The swap shows each one in percent and dollars.

How are venues compared?+

For your exact amount, Skiff asks each venue for a route, then runs every route's transaction in a simulation on the same Robinhood Chain block, from your wallet's real balance. Venues are ranked by what lands in your wallet in that simulation, not by what they quote. A route that would revert is listed as unavailable. The best route is marked MOST.

What does “+$N vs one pool” mean?+

It is how much more the best route gives than the deepest direct pool of the pair, for the same amount on the same block. Both are simulated, not quoted. Example: $100,000 USDG into NVDA at block 115,251,744 (15 Nov 2026, 21:17 UTC): best route 602.26 NVDA, the Uniswap V3 NVDA/USDG 0.05% pool 597.55 NVDA, +$778.49. We show the number only when it is positive. At smaller sizes one pool often gives as much or more, and then you see no number. When no single pool comes close (3% or more behind), we say the order is too big for one pool instead of showing a dollar figure.

Where does my money go?+

Before you sign, the route shows which pools fill your order and what share each takes, for example 57% Uniswap V3 · 19% Uniswap V4 · 7% Aero. The shares are read from the simulation of your swap. Fills by market makers and prop AMMs that don't emit a pool event are grouped as other liquidity.

Can I pay from another chain?+

Yes. Pay with USDC, USDT or the native coin on Base, Arbitrum, Ethereum, Optimism, BNB Chain, Polygon or Solana. The route bridges and buys, and the stock token arrives on Robinhood Chain. Cross-chain routes are compared by quote, because the bridge leg runs after you sign and can't be simulated ahead. The bridge, its fee and its time estimate are shown on the route.

Does Skiff hold my funds?+

No. You sign every transaction in your own wallet, and it goes from your wallet to the venue's contract. There is no deposit, no balance with us and no contract of ours holding your money. Approvals are for the exact amount of each swap, never unlimited.

What is “1 signature”?+

When your wallet supports it, the approval and the swap are signed together and land as one transaction. Otherwise you sign two transactions in a row: approve, then swap. Same route, same amounts.

What is a solver fill?+

A route where your transaction locks the input and a solver delivers the stock token right after. It can't be simulated ahead, so it is listed apart from the ranked venues. Its main number is the guaranteed minimum: the least the solver must deliver.

Who can buy stock tokens?+

Stock tokens are not available to US persons and are restricted in some other countries. Check what applies where you live before you buy. Skiff shows quotes and builds transactions you sign yourself. It is not a broker and sells nothing of its own.

What can go wrong?+

Quotes change between the block they were simulated on and the block your transaction lands in. If the price moves past your slippage limit, the swap reverts: your tokens stay in your wallet and only network gas is spent. A route can also stop working between quote and signature; get a fresh quote and try again. Cross-chain transfers take as long as the bridge takes. Pools trade around the clock, so when a stock's market is closed, pool prices can drift from the last close. Quotes are data, not advice.

Do I need an account?+

No. Quotes, the board and big trades work without one. To swap, connect your wallet. Your watchlist and alerts are saved against your wallet address.
