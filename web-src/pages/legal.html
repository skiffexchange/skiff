Privacy

Without a wallet

You can use quotes, the board and big trades without connecting anything. Your IP address is used for a few minutes to limit request rates and is not kept with any profile.

With a wallet

When you connect, we store your wallet address, your watchlist, your alerts and the transaction hashes of swaps you add to history. Wallet sign-in is handled by Privy.

What we don't do

We don't sell data, run ad trackers or ask for your name or email. Everything you sign is public on chain by nature.

Deleting your data

Remove your watchlist, alerts and Telegram link in the app, and they are deleted.
